------------------------------------------

`org.jetbrains.kotlin:kotlin-compiler-embeddable` 2.4.10, which Xide uses as a library dependency. Kotlin is licensed
under the Apache License 2.0:
https://github.com/JetBrains/kotlin/blob/master/license/LICENSE.txt

Xide. `third_party/intellij-groovy-psi/UPSTREAM.md` lists every file and every change. The files are licensed under
the Apache License 2.0, see `LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`:
